Privacy Policy for SplitTrip

Last updated: July 17, 2026

SplitTrip is designed with a privacy-first approach. The app does not require an account or user registration.

Data Usage

All trips, expenses, participants, balances, and other user-created data are stored locally on your device.

SplitTrip does not collect or store your personal data on its own servers.

AI Receipt Scan

If you choose to use the AI Receipt Scan feature, the receipt image you select is securely transmitted to our AI processing service solely to extract expense information from the receipt.

Receipt images are processed only for this purpose and are not used to identify you, create user profiles, or deliver advertising.

The AI Receipt Scan feature is completely optional. You can always add expenses manually without using AI.

Purchases

SplitTrip offers optional Premium subscriptions that are processed securely through Apple's StoreKit.

We do not receive or store your payment information. All purchases, billing, subscriptions, free trials, and renewals are managed by Apple.

What We Do Not Do

We do not:

  • Require user accounts
  • Collect personal profiles
  • Track users across apps or websites
  • Use advertising SDKs
  • Use analytics services
  • Sell personal information

Third-Party Services

SplitTrip does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

The optional AI Receipt Scan feature uses third-party AI processing solely to analyze receipt images requested by the user.

Security

Your trips, expenses, participants, balances, and other app data remain stored locally on your device.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date.
