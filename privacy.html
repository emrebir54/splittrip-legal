Privacy Policy for SplitTrip

Last updated: November 16, 2026

SplitTrip ("we", "our", or "the App") is operated by EEB CONSULTANCY LLC. We respect your privacy and are committed to explaining how information is collected, processed, stored, and shared when you use SplitTrip.

SplitTrip helps users organize shared trips, track expenses, calculate balances, and manage settlements. The App does not require account registration.

This Privacy Policy describes our data practices, including optional AI-powered features, cloud synchronization, advertising, and third-party services.

1. Information We Process

Trip and Expense Information

SplitTrip processes information you enter or generate while using the App, which may include:

  • Trip names and related trip information
  • Participant names
  • Expense descriptions, amounts, and categories
  • Expense splits and participant balances
  • Settlement records and payment calculations
  • Shared trip information and synchronization data

This information is stored locally on your device. When cloud synchronization or shared-trip functionality is used, relevant information is also transmitted to and stored on our backend infrastructure.

Please avoid entering sensitive personal information that is not necessary for expense management.

Device Identifiers

SplitTrip may generate and store an application-specific device identifier to support synchronization and application functionality without requiring an account.

When push notifications are enabled, the App may process an Apple Push Notification service (APNs) device token to deliver relevant notifications.

Advertising services may separately process device identifiers and advertising identifiers as described in the Advertising section below.

2. Cloud Synchronization

SplitTrip uses Cloudflare-based backend infrastructure to support shared trips, synchronization, and related application features.

Depending on the features you use, our backend may process and store trip information, participant names, expenses, settlements, device identifiers, and notification tokens.

This information is used to operate the App, maintain shared-trip consistency, and deliver requested functionality.

We do not provide your trip or expense records to advertising providers for targeted advertising.

Information may remain in backend records after it is removed from an active trip, including records marked for deletion or synchronization purposes.

You may contact us to request deletion of personal information associated with your use of SplitTrip. Requests will be evaluated and fulfilled in accordance with applicable law, including any necessary retention obligations.

3. AI Receipt Scan

SplitTrip offers an optional AI Receipt Scan feature that helps extract expense information from receipt images.

When you choose to scan a receipt, the selected image is transmitted through our Cloudflare backend to Google's Gemini AI service for processing.

Extracted information may include merchant names, purchased items, prices, totals, and other information visible on the receipt.

Receipt images and extracted content may be processed by our service providers under their applicable data-processing and retention terms. Technical logs may also contain processing information.

We do not intentionally use receipt images to identify you or build advertising profiles. However, receipts may contain personal information you choose to include, and third-party processing is subject to the relevant provider's terms.

The AI Receipt Scan feature is optional. You can always add expenses manually without using AI.

4. Advertising and Google AdMob

SplitTrip uses Google AdMob to display advertisements to users who do not have Premium access.

Advertisements may include banner ads, interstitial ads, and app open ads.

Premium subscribers receive an ad-free experience within SplitTrip.

Google AdMob and its advertising partners may automatically collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising and device identifiers
  • IP addresses and approximate location derived from them
  • Advertising interactions and impressions
  • App usage and product interaction information
  • Performance, crash, and diagnostic information

This information may be used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, analytics, and, where permitted, personalized advertising.

Google and its partners process advertising data according to their own privacy practices and applicable legal requirements.

Learn more about Google's data practices: Google Privacy Policy .

You can also learn how Google uses information from apps and sites that use its services: Google's Partner Services Information .

5. Advertising Consent and Tracking

GDPR and Consent Management

SplitTrip uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage applicable advertising consent requirements, including for users in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, and Switzerland.

Where required, you may be asked to consent to personalized advertising or manage your advertising preferences.

Where applicable, you can review or change your advertising privacy choices through the Privacy Options feature in the App's Settings.

Declining personalized advertising does not necessarily remove advertisements. Non-personalized or other legally permitted advertising may still be displayed.

Apple App Tracking Transparency

On supported Apple devices, SplitTrip may request permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework before allowing tracking across apps or websites owned by other companies.

You can decline this permission or change your selection in your device's privacy settings.

ATT permission and GDPR advertising consent are separate mechanisms. Granting one does not automatically grant the other.

We respect applicable consent requirements and device-level tracking permissions.

6. Push Notifications

SplitTrip may use Apple Push Notification service to deliver notifications related to shared trips and application functionality.

If you enable notifications, a device push token may be transmitted to our backend for notification delivery.

You can disable notifications at any time through your device's system settings.

7. Exchange Rate Services

SplitTrip may retrieve currency exchange rates from external providers, including Frankfurter and ExchangeRate-API services.

These requests may expose technical information, such as your IP address, to the relevant provider as part of normal network communication.

These services are used to support currency conversion and related expense calculations.

8. Purchases and Premium

SplitTrip offers optional Premium subscriptions processed through Apple's StoreKit.

Payments, subscription billing, renewals, and applicable trials are managed by Apple. We do not receive your full payment card details.

The App may process subscription entitlement information to determine whether Premium features and the ad-free experience are available.

Premium subscribers are not served Google AdMob advertisements by SplitTrip while their Premium entitlement is active.

9. Data Sharing and Third Parties

SplitTrip uses third-party service providers to operate specific application features.

  • Cloudflare for backend infrastructure and synchronization
  • Google Gemini for optional AI receipt processing
  • Google AdMob for advertising and advertising measurement
  • Google UMP for advertising consent management
  • Apple for purchases and push notification delivery
  • External exchange-rate providers for currency conversion

We share or transmit information to these providers as necessary for the relevant features and subject to their applicable privacy practices.

We do not sell your trip records, participant names, or expense details to data brokers.

Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its partners for the purposes described above, subject to your applicable privacy choices.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it or remove the App, subject to device backups and system behavior.

Information synchronized to our backend may remain for as long as needed to provide shared-trip functionality, maintain data consistency, resolve technical issues, or meet applicable legal obligations.

Deleting content from the App may not immediately remove every corresponding backend record.

You may request access to or deletion of personal information by contacting us at info@eebconsultancyllc.com .

We may need sufficient information to verify your request and identify the relevant records, especially because SplitTrip does not require user accounts.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information processed through SplitTrip, including secure network communication and access controls.

No electronic storage or transmission method can guarantee absolute security.

You should avoid sharing sensitive information unnecessarily in trip names, participant names, expense descriptions, or receipt images.

12. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than your country of residence.

Where applicable, international processing and transfers are subject to relevant legal requirements and safeguards.

13. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or other control over your personal information.

You may also have the right to object to certain processing activities or withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

To exercise applicable rights, contact us at info@eebconsultancyllc.com .

You can also manage certain advertising choices through the App's Privacy Options screen and Apple's device privacy settings.

14. Children's Privacy

SplitTrip is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

If you believe a child has provided personal information that requires removal, please contact us.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in application features, third-party services, legal requirements, or data practices.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.
