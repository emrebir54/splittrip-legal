Privacy Policy

SplitTrip is designed with a privacy-first approach and does not require an account or user registration.

All trips, expenses, participants, balances, and other user-created data are stored locally on your device.

SplitTrip does not collect or store your personal data on its own servers.

The optional AI Receipt Scan feature securely transmits the receipt image you select to our AI processing service solely to extract expense information from the receipt.

Receipt images are processed only for this purpose and are not used to identify you, create user profiles, or deliver advertising.

SplitTrip offers optional Premium subscriptions processed securely through Apple's StoreKit. We do not receive or store your payment information.

SplitTrip does not use advertising SDKs, analytics services, or user tracking.
